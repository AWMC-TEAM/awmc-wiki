## 🚀 AWMC Bot in Three Minutes

To quickly set up the bot service, please visit:
[AWMC BOT](./bot/intro.md)

1. **Add the bot** — Click the link above, or have a group owner invite the bot to a group.
   For the best group experience (song guessing, queue cards, etc.), allow the bot to read all
   messages in group settings.
2. **Agree to the terms** — Send `用户协议`, read the web page, and reply with the full
   confirmation phrase.
3. **Bind your maimai account** — Send `mai绑定`, then send the `SGWCMAID...` text obtained by
   recognizing the "舞萌DX | 中二节奏" QR code in WeChat.
4. **Query scores** — Send `b50` for your score chart. For Diving Fish / Lxns binding or
   AWMC NET binding-free lookup, see [Basic Tutorial](/en/guide/bot/intro).
5. **Group features & management** — Use guessing games, check-ins and queue cards directly in
   the group. Group owners / admins can send `群管理` to open the
   [Group Console](/en/guide/bot/group-console) for feature switches, group stats, arcade
   queues and weather.

See [Command Reference](/en/guide/bot/advanced) for the full command list and
[Commands](/en/guide/bot/commands) for account / upload details.
