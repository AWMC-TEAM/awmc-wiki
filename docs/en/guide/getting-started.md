# AWMC TEAM Services

::: tip ☕ Support AWMC
AWMC is a community-driven **semi-nonprofit project**. Core features are **free forever**, and we charge **no mandatory fees**. Keeping them online costs real money: if AWMC has helped you, please consider [sponsoring us](/en/sponsor) — every bit of support keeps the services stable. Part of sponsorship income also goes to **charitable donations**, with income and expenses published regularly.

That said, please **spend responsibly** — only chip in what you can afford. 🐾
:::

We provide numerous open services and APIs for you to use.
For some APIs, you need to apply for access.
You can contact our team by sending an email via the icon above.
