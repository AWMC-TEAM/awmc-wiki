# ☕ Sponsor AWMC

All AWMC services are maintained by community members in their spare time. There is **no mandatory fee** — every core feature stays free, forever.

Keeping them online costs real money. If AWMC has ever helped you — looked up a score, saved you a wasted trip to the arcade, or just kept you company in the group chat — consider buying us a coffee.

Every bit of your support, you'll see exactly where it went.

::: tip 💛 Please spend responsibly
Thank you for all the love and support!

That said, please **spend responsibly** — only chip in what you can comfortably afford.

Thank you for loving AWMC! :heart:
:::

## What We Run

| Service | Description |
| --- | --- |
| [AWMC Bot](/en/guide/bot/intro) | maimai DX score lookup, rating calculation, chart search & more |
| [maimai Status Page](https://status.awmc.cc) | Server monitoring across all ISPs (CMCC / CT / CU), refreshed every 20s |
| [Public API](/en/dev/) | Open interfaces for third-party developers |
| [Chart Preview](/en/dev/chart-preview) | Online chart viewing and analysis |

## Where the Money Goes

Sponsorship income flows in two directions: **keeping the services alive**, and **passing the kindness on**.

| Item | Description |
| --- | --- |
| 🖥️ Servers | Hosting the bot, APIs, databases and status monitoring |
| 🌐 Domains & Certificates | Annual renewals and SSL certificates |
| 📡 Monitoring Nodes | Multi-ISP probe nodes for the status page (Shanghai CMCC / CT / CU) |
| 🚀 CDN & Bandwidth | Static asset delivery, chart and image acceleration |
| 🔧 Third-party Services | Object storage, image recognition and other metered APIs |
| 💛 Charity | A portion of sponsorship income, donated to charity — see the [AWMC Charity board](https://bbs.wmc.pub/forums/awmc.16/) |

::: tip Cost Transparency
According to the public [sponsor list thread](https://awmc.cc/threads/91/post-267) on AWMC BBS, keeping the services stable costs **¥1650.00 CNY**.

For detailed and regularly updated breakdowns, please refer to the original thread.
:::

::: tip Your Support Finds Its Way Back
Your support keeps more than just our servers alive.

AWMC puts part of its sponsorship income toward **charity** — so it reaches the people who need it most. :heart:
:::

## How to Sponsor

Your support is more than welcome. If you enjoy what we do, feel free to treat us — even just one round of maimai! 🎮

### Option 1: Afdian (Recommended)

Monthly sponsorship — sustainable support with visible goals and progress.

::: tip Why Afdian?
Afdian is **public and transparent**: every record is traceable, and recurring monthly support helps far more with long-term maintenance than one-off transfers.
